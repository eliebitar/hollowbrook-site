Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026

The short version: Hollowbrook does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you create in the game stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Hollowbrook has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no servers that the game talks to.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • The name and leaderboard alias you type during onboarding, your character's appearance and your questionnaire answers
  • Your current run, scores and other game statistics

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your final score is submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboard so you can compare it with other players. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive any of your Game Center information. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in the Settings app.

Children

Hollowbrook is rated 17+ and is not intended for children. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party SDKs, analytics tools or advertising networks.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
