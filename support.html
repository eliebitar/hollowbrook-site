How to play

What happens each night?

Every night is one level in four phases. At dusk you walk the town and observe people. At night you follow the person you chose and act only somewhere dark with no one watching. Before dawn you hide what happened, clean up any clues and get home. The next day you answer Detective Calder's questions and join the search party.

How do I move and interact?

Drag the joystick on the left to walk. When you can do something – observe someone, carry, hide, clean up – a button appears on the right. Some actions ask you to hold the button until its ring fills.

Why can't I choose the person I observed?

Each profile only targets a certain kind of person, and the game won't let you break that pattern. Read your character's thoughts on the observation card – they tell you what you're looking for.

What raises suspicion?

Being seen with the person who goes missing, being spotted by a patrol, answers that contradict what witnesses saw, clues the searchers find, and wandering away from the search party. Suspicion falls slowly on quiet days. If it reaches 100%, you're arrested.

What are the sanity and "voices" effects?

Some profiles have a sanity meter. As it drops, the game behaves differently – you may hear things, lose time or see people who aren't there. That's intentional: it's how each condition reveals itself.

How do I unlock a case file?

From the second night, the end-of-day journal lets you guess your character's diagnosis once. Guess correctly for bonus points and an unlocked case file. Every case file also opens when a run ends.

Profile, progress and leaderboard

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your run is saved at the start of every night. If you quit mid-night, you'll restart that night next time.

Can I get a different profile?

On the home screen, tap Retake questionnaire. This erases your profile and on-device leaderboard so you can start fresh.

How does the leaderboard work?

Every finished run is recorded on your device's Hall of Infamy. If you're signed in to Game Center, your score also goes to the global leaderboard.

Content and wellbeing

Is Hollowbrook based on real people?

The characters and the town are fictional. Each profile's symptoms and patterns are modelled on a documented historical case, which the game describes factually in its case files. The game does not depict real victims.

I'm finding the themes difficult.

Please take a break. Most people living with mental illness are never violent and are far more likely to be victims of violence. If you're struggling, in the US you can call or text 988; elsewhere, find a local helpline at findahelpline.com.
